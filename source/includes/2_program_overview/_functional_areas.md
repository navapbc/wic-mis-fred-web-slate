## 2.3 Summary of Functional Areas

There are twelve (12) WIC information system functional areas:

- Certification
- Nutrition Education and Health Surveillance and Referrals
- Food Management
- Food Benefit Issuance
- Food Benefit Redemption, Settlement, and Reconciliation
- Financial Management
- Caseload Management
- Operations Management
- Vendor Management
- Scheduling
- System Administration
- Reporting
