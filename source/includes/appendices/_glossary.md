## Appendix 1: WIC MIS Glossary

**Advance Planning Document (APD)**: The Advance Planning Document (APD) process is a series of successive steps through which State agencies obtain federal prior approval of and federal financial participation (FFP) in automation projects supporting FNS programs. This includes all eligibility system and Electronic Benefit Transfer (EBT) projects.

**Application Programming Interface (API)**: APIs are the mechanisms through which individual software applications pass information to one another. Communications between the systems are facilitated by API calls, which are simply requests between the application to send new information to another system, or ask for updated information from another System.

**Broadbanding**: In the context of a WIC food package, which is broadbanded when the food is issued at its broader category (such as Whole Grains) rather than a specific subcategory (such as whole wheat pasta), allowing participants to make choices at the store based on preference and availability.

**Chatbot**: Software that provides customer service or other information by simulating conversation with human agents, either through rule-based interaction or a more advanced AI combination of natural language processing and machine learning.

**Clinic**: A location operated by the WIC local agency to provide WIC services.

**Consortium**: Group of State agencies that share MIS code, enabling cost sharing for improvements and maintenance. Changes to the MIS typically require a majority vote by State agencies in the consortium. MIS consortia can either be SAM or non-SAM.

**Contractor**: Technology company providing MIS maintenance and operations, building out services that integrate with the MIS for agencies (e.g., Teletask, WICShopper, TrueNorth), or otherwise providing technology services to State agencies.

**Emerging technology**: Technology largely new to the world, which is not widely understood and which carries uncertain risks, such as generative artificial intelligence (AI). This technology is distinct from more established technologies which may be underutilized by the WIC program, such as optical character recognition or mobile-friendly web portals.

**Food Delivery Portal (FDP)**: The federal system that WIC State agencies use to report on WIC Vendor Management activities. Previously known as The Integrity Profile (TIP).

**Functional Requirements Document (FReD)**: This document, the Functional Requirements Document that details a comprehensive description of functions that can be automated to support the WIC program.

**Governance**: A set of rules, procedures, and standards that guide how to manage a technical system, such as WIC MIS

**Interoperability**: Refers to the ability of different systems or components—potentially from different sources—to work together, exchange information, and use that information meaningfully. In this report, this refers to the ability for a system or component to readily exchange information with the MIS.

**Indian Tribal Organization (ITO)**: An Indian Tribal Organization (ITO) is the formally recognized governing entity of a Tribe, Tribal advocacy group, or consortium of Tribes, that may take on the responsibilities of a State agency such as participation in SNAP or food distribution programs.
